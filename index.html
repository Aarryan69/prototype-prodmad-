The 4-Stage Active Pedagogy Pathway (Slide 05)

Anti-Cheat by Design
Phase 1

01 [Explore]

Children observe familiar harvest context (24 tomatoes, 6 baskets) without pre-calculated answers or formulas.

"Attempt before hint: System withholds prompts until student makes an authentic trial."
Phase 2

02 [Attempt]

Active tactile and digital distribution. Non-judgmental nudge highlights unequal groups rather than giving the solution.

"Hint is a scaffold, not the answer: AI acts as a mirror, never an automated answer engine."
Phase 3

03 [Explain]

Students articulate to group mates and teacher why the distribution works ("24 divided among 6 means 4 each") in local dialect.

"Retention solidifies through verbal justification and social peer learning."
Phase 4

04 [Transfer]

Solving novel transfer variations (e.g. 30 tomatoes across 5 baskets) unassisted on paper or slate to prove true cognitive transfer.

"Guarantees conceptual mastery over rote memory or passive guessing."
